How it worksonce a round, every five minutes
  1. Fees come in

    Every $ROTATOR trade pays a fee. StonkFun sends the creator's share, 0.5% of volume, to the payout wallet, part in SOL and part in ROTATOR.

  2. The stock turns

    Every five minutes the next stock takes its turn: AAPL, TSLA, NVDA, MSFT, AMZN, and around. The schedule is fixed from launch, so anyone can compute it. This page does.

  3. Holders are counted

    Once a round, at a random moment nobody can time, every ROTATOR balance is read. The new SOL, less a 5% ops cut, is credited to holders by the size of their holding.

  4. Paid in that stock

    Anyone whose credit reaches 0.005 SOL is paid. The keeper buys that round's stock on Jupiter and sends it to their wallet. Smaller credits carry over and keep adding up.

  5. All of it on chain

    The rounds above are read from the payout wallet's own transactions, not from a log we keep. Every buy and every payment links to the chain.

What you are trustingread this before you buy
A keeper pays, not a contract

Payouts are made by a server that holds the payout wallet's key. If it stops, payouts stop. Credits not yet paid are kept on the keeper's ledger, not on chain. What has been paid is on chain and shown above.

xStocks are not offered to US persons read

The stocks paid out are xStocks, tracker certificates issued by Backed. Backed does not offer them to US persons or to residents of several other countries, including Canada and the UK. Stonk Rotator cannot see where holders live. Check whether you may hold them. Kraken's xStocks terms

The issuer can freeze or take back the stock

Every xStock is a Token-2022 token on which Backed holds a permanent delegate, which can move or burn tokens in any wallet, and the power to pause all transfers. None is in use today. The keeper checks for a pause or new restrictions each round and stops paying that stock if it finds one. Credits keep building.

No volume, no payouts

Payouts are funded only by trading fees. Nothing else pays into the wallet. A quiet day means small rounds, and a dead coin means none. The stock is bought at market, and the keeper refuses any swap that moves the price more than 2%.

Your first payment in a stock costs rent

If your wallet has never held a stock, the first payment in it opens an account for it. About 0.0016 SOL of rent comes out of your own credit. The account is yours, and closing it gives the rent back. Network fees are paid from the ops cut, not from holders.

Who is left out

The payout, ops and burn wallets, and every program-owned account: the bonding curve, pool vaults and lockers. Fees that arrive as ROTATOR stay in the payout wallet. The keeper never sells them and never pays them out.
