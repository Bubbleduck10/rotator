how it worksand what would break it

The index is applied to the token side of the curve, never the quote side. Both give a price proportional to the index, but scaling the quote side makes the pool promise SOL it does not hold: at an index of 2 a large sell demands 181% of the reserve, at 10 it demands 909%. Scaling the token side bounds the payout by construction.

At an index of exactly 1.0 the two are identical. That is what makes it dangerous — the wrong one looks perfectly correct until the pairing has actually moved.

When a trade would exceed what the curve can honour, the quote returns null and this page says so, rather than filling you short and keeping the difference.
